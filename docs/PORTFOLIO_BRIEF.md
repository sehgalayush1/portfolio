# Portfolio Brief

## Status and purpose

Approved on 2026-10-18 as the outcome of Phase 2 Task 1.

This brief records the agreed audience, positioning, and primary visitor action. Use it to guide content curation, page copy, and the later visual-design document. It establishes direction, not final website copy.

See [PROJECT_PLAN.md](./PROJECT_PLAN.md) for the phased scope and [PHASE_2_TASKS.md](./PHASE_2_TASKS.md) for the task checklist.

## Goals

- **Near term:** attract serious recruiters and engineering hiring managers who will consider the portfolio owner for relevant hiring opportunities.
- **Long term:** grow into a lasting career repository without losing a curated, focused first impression.

## Audience

Recruiters and engineering hiring managers at FAANG-level companies and successful startups.

- Recruiters should be able to quickly understand the engineering profile, experience, and potential fit.
- Engineering hiring managers should find evidence of technical judgment, ownership, depth, and impact.

## Target opportunities

Senior individual-contributor and hands-on technical-lead roles in backend engineering with product ownership.

Emphasize hands-on design and implementation, ownership of product and project outcomes, and the ability to guide technical delivery. Do not position the portfolio primarily for people-management roles.

These are target opportunities, not claims about a current title or equivalent levels across employers.

## Positioning and main message

Lead with backend engineering and product ownership, rather than a platform-first or AI-first specialization.

> I build backend systems that solve real product problems, owning the work from design through delivery and production - with the ability to guide a team along the way.

Supporting evidence should demonstrate:

- Product outcomes and the engineering work behind them.
- System-design decisions and trade-offs.
- Reliability and responsibility for systems in production.
- Technical leadership and helping a team deliver.

Platform and distributed-systems depth, developer tooling, and applied AI reinforce this story rather than define the headline.

## Primary visitor action

**Explore my work.**

Lead visitors toward selected project stories that provide evidence beyond the resume. Keep resume and contact links readily available so visitors can also assess fit or start a hiring conversation directly.

## Presentation principles

- Present a concise introduction and selected project stories before an exhaustive career history.
- Complement the resume rather than duplicate it.
- Explain the problem, personal contribution, decisions, trade-offs, and outcomes behind selected work.
- Let the longer-term career repository grow without giving every item equal prominence.
